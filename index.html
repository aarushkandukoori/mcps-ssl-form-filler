MCPS SSL Hours Form Filler

Fill out MCPS Form 560-51 — Student Service Learning Activity Verification — right in your browser, download the completed PDF, and email it to your counselor or SSL coordinator.

Unofficial student-made tool · Not affiliated with or endorsed by Montgomery County Public Schools

🔒 Private by design: everything happens in your browser. Nothing you type is ever uploaded to a server. Your answers are saved only on this device so you can come back later.
Section I

Student Information

You (the student) fill this out.

Section II

Nonprofit Organization Information

Officially completed by your supervisor at the organization. If they gave you the details (or you're preparing the form for them to sign), fill in what you know — anything left blank stays blank on the PDF.

If “No,” MCPS Form 560-50 (pre-approval) must be attached. Check the MCVC list.

Service Record

Max 8 in a 24-hour period

The supervisor's signature itself must be added by hand (or with a PDF signing tool) — this site can't sign for them.

Section III

Student Reflection

Write one paragraph answering these — the SSL coordinator will return the form if it's incomplete:

  • What did you do, and what need did your service address?
  • Who benefitted from your service?
  • What did you learn about yourself, and how did helping others make you feel?
  • How was this experience connected to something you learned in a class at school?
  • What skills did you use or build on that could help you with a future career?

0 characters — the box on the printed form fits roughly 900–1,100 characters. Longer reflections shrink to fit; if it gets tiny, attach a separate page instead.

✉️ Send it to your counselor

Enter your counselor's (or SSL coordinator's) email — we'll open a ready-to-send draft for you.

Don't know it? Ask your school's front office, or find your SSL coordinator on the MCPS SSL page. Most staff emails end in @mcpsmd.org.

✅ Your form is ready!

  1. Download your completed PDF (it may have downloaded automatically).
  2. Email it — tap a button below to open a pre-written draft to your counselor, then attach the downloaded PDF before hitting send. (Browsers can't attach files automatically — don't forget this step!)

Remember: your supervisor still needs to sign Section II before the form is officially complete. Many students email the filled form to their supervisor first, get it signed, then send it to their counselor/SSL coordinator.
